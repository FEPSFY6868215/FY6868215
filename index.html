There are different types of hydrogen, the main ones being Green, Grey and Blue, we will be mainly focusing on Green,
                    it is produced by electrolysis from renewable sources involves breaking down water molecules (H2O) 
                    into oxygen (O2) and hydrogen (H2) (Iberdrola, 2022) . This is the general overview but to delve further 
                    to first obtain Hydrogen there are three main steps: Molecular Transformation; Gasification and Water 
                    Electrolysis. In the initial stages a high temperature water vapour is used to separate the carbon from 
                    the hydrogen that makes up the natural gas (www.acciona.com, n.d.) to obtain dihydrogen and carbon dioxide. 
                    Then in Gasification, the biomass is burned in a reactor at high temperatures (US Department of Energy, 2019) 
                    to make a desired yield. Finally, the products undergo water electrolysis to separate the hydrogen completely 
                    with the help of direct electrical current which connects to the anode and cathode via the water.
